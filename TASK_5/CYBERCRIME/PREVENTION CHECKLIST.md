 Stay Safe Online - 10 Actionable Tips for Indian College Students

DAILY SAFETY CHECKLIST:
-Never share UPI PIN - No bank/Paytm calls for "verification" EVER
-Enable UPI biometric lock - Fingerprint/Face ID for every PhonePe/Google Pay transaction
- App lock ALL payment apps - Pattern/PIN/Fingerprint protection
- Verify sender before replying - Check WhatsApp profile pics, hover over email IDs
- Social media = PRIVATE - Only VIT batchmates/family see posts
- 2FA everywhere - Gmail, college email, LinkedIn, GitHub
- Change passwords weekly - 12+ characters with numbers/symbols
- No public WiFi banking - Use mobile hotspot for UPI/SBI YONO
